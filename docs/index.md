# Mailbell for Gmail

A Firefox and Chrome toolbar extension that tells you when new Gmail arrives and lets you read, archive, delete or unsubscribe without opening Gmail.

Mailbell signs in with Google and talks only to the official Gmail API, straight from your browser. It has no server of its own, no analytics and no telemetry.

- Desktop notifications for new mail, per account, with quiet hours.
- Read whole threads, reply, archive, delete, mark as read, report spam or unsubscribe from the toolbar.
- Several Google accounts side by side.
- One-time codes from verification emails, ready to copy.
