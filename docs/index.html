Your Gmail, from the toolbar. Nothing in between.

Mailbell tells you when mail arrives and lets you read, reply, archive and unsubscribe without opening Gmail. It has no server: your mail goes only between your browser and Google.

Download Source code

Free for Firefox 140+ and Chrome 120+, including Edge and Brave.

Private by design

Mailbell is built so there is nothing to trust but your browser and Google.

No server
It talks to the official Gmail API straight from your browser. No analytics, no telemetry, no account.
No tracking pixels
Remote images stay hidden until you allow them, so senders can't see when you open their mail.
Warns about fakes
Spoofed names, lookalike domains, Spam and mail that fails sender checks open as plain text, every link shown in full.
Open source
Every line is on GitHub under GPL-3.0. Your sign-in and cached mail stay on your device.

Everything you do with mail, quicker

The everyday jobs, a click away, for every account you have.

Notifications
For all mail, Primary only or Important only, per account, with quiet hours and your own sound.
A full reader in a tab
Folders, labels, search, All inboxes across accounts, and a reading pane, with Gmail's shortcuts.
One-click actions
Archive, delete, mark read, report spam, reply or unsubscribe, for one thread or many, with undo.
Codes and meetings
One-time codes ready to copy, and Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex links you can join in one click.
Your colours
Light, dark or following your system, in any colour or a two-colour gradient.
