Your Gmail, from the toolbar. Nothing in between.

Mailbell is a free, open-source Gmail notifier and reader for Firefox and Chrome. It tells you when mail arrives and lets you read, reply, archive and unsubscribe without opening Gmail. It has no server: your mail goes only between your browser and Google.

Download Source code

Private by design

No server, no tracking

Mailbell talks to the official Gmail API straight from your browser. No analytics, no telemetry, no account.

Blocks tracking pixels

Remote images stay hidden until you allow them, so senders can't see when you open their mail.

Warns about fakes

Spoofed names, lookalike domains, Spam and mail that fails sender checks open as plain text, links shown in full.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under GPL-3.0. Your sign-in tokens and cached mail stay on your device.

Everything you do with mail, quicker

Notifications

For all mail, Primary only or Important only, per account, with quiet hours and your own sound.

A full reader in a tab

Folders, labels, search, All inboxes across accounts, and a reading pane, with Gmail's keyboard shortcuts.

One-click actions

Archive, delete, mark read, report spam, reply or unsubscribe, for one thread or many, with undo.

Codes and meetings

One-time codes ready to copy, and Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex links you can join in one click.

All features

Works with

Firefox 140 and newer, and Chrome 120 and newer (Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers too). Any Gmail or Google Workspace account, several at once.
