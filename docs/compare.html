How Mailbell compares

There are three usual ways to keep up with Gmail besides Mailbell. Each is a fine choice for someone; here is where Mailbell differs.

Mailbell A Gmail tab Notifier extensions that use your Gmail session Desktop mail apps
Notifications from the toolbar Yes Only while the tab is open Yes Yes, outside the browser
Read, reply and act without opening Gmail Yes - Varies Yes
How it reaches your mail Official Gmail API, with access you grant and can revoke Gmail itself Often by reading Gmail's web page with your browser's sign-in IMAP or the Gmail API
A server between you and Google None None Varies Some route mail through their own servers
Tracking pixels blocked by default Yes Loaded on open by default, through Google's proxy Varies Varies
Open source Yes, GPL-3.0 No Varies Some
Cost Free Free Free or subscription Free to subscription

When something else fits better

  • You write long mail all day: Gmail itself or a desktop app has the full editor; Mailbell replies in plain text.
  • You use mail outside Google: Mailbell is Gmail only. A desktop app handles any provider.
  • You want mail on your phone: Mailbell lives in your desktop browser.
