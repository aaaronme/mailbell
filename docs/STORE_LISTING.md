# Store listing text

The copy for addons.mozilla.org and the Chrome Web Store, kept here so both stores say the same thing. Not part of the website (`_config.yml` excludes it).

## Name

Mailbell for Gmail

## Summary

AMO allows 250 characters; the Chrome Web Store's short description allows 132. This one fits both:

> Private, open-source Gmail notifier and reader. Read, reply, archive and unsubscribe from the toolbar. No server, no tracking.

## Description

> Mailbell tells you when new Gmail arrives and lets you deal with it from your browser's toolbar, without opening Gmail.
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> • No server, no analytics, no telemetry. Your mail goes only between your browser and Google, through the official Gmail API.
> • Blocks tracking pixels: remote images stay hidden until you allow them.
> • Warns about fakes: Spam, mail that fails sender checks, spoofed names and lookalike domains open as plain text, with every link's full address shown.
> • Open source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/aaaronme/mailbell
>
> EVERYTHING YOU DO WITH MAIL, QUICKER
> • Notifications for all mail, Primary only or Important only, per account, with quiet hours and sounds.
> • A full mail client in a tab: folders, labels, search, All inboxes across accounts, and a reading pane.
> • Archive, delete, mark read, report spam, reply and unsubscribe in one click, with undo.
> • Gmail's keyboard shortcuts.
> • One-time codes ready to copy; join Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex in one click.
> • Several Gmail and Google Workspace accounts at once.
> • Light, dark or system theme, in any colour.
>
> Google shows an "unverified app" screen at sign-in until Mailbell completes Google's review: choose Advanced, then continue.
>
> Privacy policy: https://aaaronme.github.io/mailbell/privacy
