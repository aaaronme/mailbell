Features

Mailbell does the everyday Gmail jobs from your browser's toolbar, and keeps your mail private while it does.

Notifications

  • Desktop notifications for new unread mail: for all mail, Primary only or Important only.
  • On or off per account, with quiet hours.
  • Four built-in sounds, or your own file in Firefox, chosen per account.
  • Click a notification to open that mail.

Reading and writing

  • The toolbar popup lists new mail; open a thread to read all of it, HTML mail and attachments included.
  • The mail client tab: accounts, folders and labels on the left, the list in the middle, the reading pane on the right. All inboxes merges every account by date.
  • Search with Gmail's own search syntax, and Primary, Promotions, Social and Updates tabs.
  • Reply and reply all in plain text, without leaving Mailbell.
  • Several Google accounts side by side, Gmail and Google Workspace alike.

Actions

  • Archive, delete, mark read or unread, report spam and change labels, for one thread or a selection.
  • Undo for every action.
  • One-click unsubscribe when the sender supports it (RFC 8058).
  • Gmail's keyboard shortcuts: j/k to move, Enter to open, e to archive, # to delete, u to go back, c to compose.

Privacy and safety

  • No server, analytics or telemetry. Mail goes only between your browser and Google.
  • Remote images and tracking pixels are blocked until you allow them, per mail or per sender.
  • The sender's verified domain is shown, from Gmail's own sender checks.
  • Mail in Spam, mail that fails sender checks, names that show a different address and lookalike domains open as plain text, with every link's full address shown before you open it.
  • A warning when replies would go to a different address.

Handy extras

  • One-time codes from verification emails, ready to copy.
  • Meeting invites: join Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex in one click, or add the event to Google Calendar.
  • Themes: light, dark or follow the system, in any colour or a two-colour gradient.
