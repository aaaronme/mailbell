Features

Mailbell does the everyday Gmail jobs from your browser's toolbar, and keeps your mail private while it does.

Notifications

What you hear about
All new mail, Primary only, or Important only.
Per account
Turn each account's notifications on or off, and give each its own sound.
Quiet hours
No notifications overnight, or whenever you choose.
Sounds
Four built in, or your own file in Firefox.
Straight to it
Click a notification to open that mail.

Reading and writing

The popup
New mail at a glance; open a thread to read all of it, HTML mail and attachments included.
The mail client tab
Accounts, folders and labels on the left, the list in the middle, the reading pane on the right.
All inboxes
Every account's inbox merged by date.
Search
Gmail's own search syntax, plus the Primary, Promotions, Social and Updates tabs.
Reply
Reply and reply all in plain text, without leaving Mailbell.
Several accounts
Gmail and Google Workspace side by side.

Actions

One click
Archive, delete, mark read or unread, report spam and change labels, for one thread or a selection.
Undo
Every action can be taken back.
Unsubscribe
One click when the sender supports it (RFC 8058).
Keyboard
Gmail's shortcuts: j k to move, Enter to open, e to archive, # to delete, u to go back, c to compose.

Privacy and safety

Nothing in between
No server, analytics or telemetry. Mail goes only between your browser and Google.
Images on your terms
Remote images and tracking pixels are blocked until you allow them, per mail or per sender.
Real sender
The sender's verified domain is shown, from Gmail's own sender checks.
Safe view
Mail in Spam, mail that fails sender checks, names that show a different address and lookalike domains open as plain text, with every link's full address shown before you open it.
Reply-to warning
You're told when replies would go to a different address.

Handy extras

One-time codes
Codes from verification emails, ready to copy.
Meetings
Join Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex in one click, or add the event to Google Calendar.
Themes
Light, dark or follow the system, in any colour or a two-colour gradient.
